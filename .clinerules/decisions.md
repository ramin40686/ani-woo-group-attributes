# Group Product Attributes — Decisions

> **Phase 2A.3 redesign (2026-11-25).** The owner clarified the product concept: GPA is a
> **reusable Global WooCommerce Attribute Template Manager** (groups are templates of
> Global Attribute definitions; loading a group adds **native** WooCommerce attribute rows;
> the product's native attribute data is the single source of truth; all attributes render
> through the standard WooCommerce/Blocksy Additional Information tab). This redesign
> **supersedes** the previous value-selection / separate-renderer architecture. New
> decisions **D28–D39** record the clarified requirements. The affected previous decisions
> are preserved below in the **Superseded / Obsolete** table with their status, the
> replacing decision, and the architectural reason. The full supersession matrix and the
> Phase 2A.2 corrections applicability assessment are in
> `reports/2026-11-25-phase-2a-3-native-attribute-template-redesign.md`. Historical
> decision text is preserved verbatim in the superseded table.
>
> **Owner decisions Q1–Q4 finalized (2026-11-27).** The owner provided final decisions on
> the four open questions (Q1–Q4) recorded after Phase 2A.3. New decisions **D40–D43**
> record these final decisions. D35 (independent top-level admin menu) is **superseded**
> by D43 (GPA submenu under Products, immediately after Attributes). D24 (group status
> policy) is **superseded** by D41 (only Published groups are loadable). The full record
> is in `reports/2026-11-27-finalize-owner-decisions-q1-q4.md`.
>
> **Owner decisions D44–D45 finalized (2026-11-27).** The owner approved the two remaining
> Phase 3.0 roadmap open items. New decision **D44** fixes the `table_style` settings UI
> location (Appearance → Customize → Anishop → Group Attributes, select control) and
> **D45** fixes the Load REST endpoint's product-ID transport (required `product_id` query
> parameter). These resolve roadmap open items OI-5 and OI-6 respectively. The full record
> is in `reports/2026-11-27-phase-3-owner-decisions-d44-d45.md`.
>
> **Owner decision D46 finalized (2026-11-28).** The owner approved relocating the GPA
> `table_style` setting from the Customizer to the GPA admin page (Dashboard → Products →
> **گروه ویژگی‌های آنی**). New decision **D46** **supersedes D44** (Customizer-control
> location): the setting now lives on the GPA admin page in a dedicated **تنظیمات نمایش**
> (display settings) section with its own form and save action, while the **گروه‌های
> ویژگی** (group management) section is reserved for Phase 3.2. The Anishop Customizer
> section is preserved as a **navigation guide** (not a settings container) with clickable
> links to the child-theme modules' configuration pages. D45 and D40–D43 remain unchanged.
> The full record is in
> `reports/2026-11-28-gpa-settings-relocation-and-customizer-guide.md`.
